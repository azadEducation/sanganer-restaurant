Who We Are

A Complete Resort & Dining Experience

Welcome to Sanganer Resort & Restaurant — Bhilwara's favourite destination for authentic Indian cuisine, refreshing café drinks, and memorable celebrations. Located on Sanganer Road near Medical College, we offer a warm, spacious environment for families, friends, and corporate guests alike.

Our 100% vegetarian menu spans Rajasthani specialties like Dal Bati, North Indian curries, Italian Pizza, South Indian Dosas, and a full café experience — all under one roof with a modern AC Hall.
